DECEPTUS GBA PREVIEW - ROUTE HELP

THE CELL
Walk right. Rats do not damage the player. Press Up at the glowing door.

CLOCKWORK ANTECHAMBER
Walk through the grandfather clock to activate its checkpoint. Jump onto the
small raised platform, then continue across the lower floor. Watch the falling
arrows near the crates. An arrow takes 60 health; a lethal hit returns to the
clock. Health is restored on death or a room restart.

Jump up the crates on the right until you reach the tall wall/overhang.
Do not keep jumping straight into the underside of the overhang. Step a little
left, jump LEFT around its edge, then steer RIGHT back toward the wall.
Release jump and press it again when touching the wall to wall-jump upward.
Repeat to climb onto the upper ledge. Continue right and press Up at the exit.

SPIKE GALLERY
Press Up while beside the starting lever. Let the spike-retraction animation
finish, walk right and jump up the raised step. Continue across the retracted
spikes, then press Up near the right end. The exit requires the lever to be off.
Select restores the lever and spikes to their initial states if needed.

CRUMBLING BRIDGE
Read the briefing before pressing Start. Walk right across the upper bridge
without waiting on a segment for more than a second. Press Up on the safe
platform at the far side to finish. If you fall, Select restarts this area.
Collapsed segments eventually return when the player is clear.

Start replays the preview after the completion screen.
