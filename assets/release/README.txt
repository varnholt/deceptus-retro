DECEPTUS - GBA PLAYABLE PREVIEW
13 November 2026

Open Deceptus-Preview.gba in your GBA emulator. The ROM is self-contained;
no source checkout, artwork folder, Python or development tools are needed.
Tested with mGBA. This package does not contain an emulator or BIOS.

CONTROLS
D-pad Left/Right: move
A or B: jump; release early for a shorter jump
Up: operate the lever / continue at an area exit
Start: begin, continue a briefing, pause/resume, or replay after finishing
Select: restart the current area

In the clockwork area, release and press jump again against a wall to wall-jump.
Each new area pauses on a briefing screen until you press Start.

WHAT TO PLAY
Four original catacomb areas form a short, curated route:
1. The cell: meet an original rat and enter the glowing door on the right.
2. Clockwork antechamber: activate the clock checkpoint, avoid arrows, and
   climb around the ledge to the upper-right exit.
3. Spike gallery: retract the spikes with the lever and cross to the right.
4. Crumbling bridge: keep moving across the upper bridge to the right.

At the end, the game shows elapsed play time, deaths and room restarts.
Rats are harmless, as in their original script. See WALKTHROUGH.txt if stuck.

PREVIEW SCOPE
This is a playable port preview, not the full game. The route is curated from
original map areas rather than the complete original progression. Sword combat,
inventory, narrative, other enemy types, audio and persistent saves are absent.
Character/world physics are a fixed-point approximation of the original.
Original artwork keeps its pixel size; GBA palette conversion remains necessary.
No physical-console test has been performed.
